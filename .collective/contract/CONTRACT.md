# Collective operating contract

This is mutable candidate content for the proposed 0.2.0 Release. It has no accepted
Release identity until the owner-authorized promotion is accepted in the authoritative
release index. Publishing candidate bytes, passing validation, and preparing a local
release directory do not establish acceptance or Program adoption.

Baseline means shared operating contract content. Release means its immutable identified
version. VOCABULARY.yaml supplies the meanings, registers and per-term remainder causes;
OBLIGATIONS.yaml supplies stable requirements. REGISTRATIONS.yaml contains only the
classified Program identity and discovery values. CLEARANCES.yaml binds manual semantic
review to exact proposed meanings and immutable receiver content. Pins describe inspected
content; they do not assert current-head compatibility or an adopted collective Release.

A meaning is admitted only after attributable manual existence and semantic review in
both structurally dissimilar registered Programs and owner authorization of this exact
candidate. Deterministic validation checks the recorded facts and bindings; it does not
resolve receivers, judge their semantics, or prove their claims. Existing receiver concepts
support the mapping; this release constructs no receiver-side referents.

Declaration and declares are reserved, without clearance or obligations. Generalized Scope,
Unit, Event, Record and Evidence grade remain deferred without exceptions or aliases.
Collective is withdrawn as an admitted term. Other local concepts remain explicitly local
or deferred. These dispositions impose no common internal workflow on either Program.

The retained obligations concern recording exactly what was adopted, preserving admitted
meanings, and changing adopted baseline-owned content through a new Release. None is observed
or enforced by this release. The declare-extensions obligation is withdrawn. There is no
structured latest-adoption or reporting obligation and no declaration mechanism here.

Repository-local workflows are not distributed as ecosystem rules by this release. Only
the closed, classified payload and integrity-bound public authorizing verdict can accompany
an accepted Release. Private decisions, evidence transcripts and local review paths remain
outside that payload. Historical 0.1.0 remains unpromoted prework. RELEASE-INTENT.yaml declares
breaking compatibility and names all seven corrections; it is authoring metadata rather
than copied release content.

An occupied release version is never rewritten, including for identical content. A defect
requires a successor version. Retrieve a release by its accepted indexed identity and verify
its complete content against the independent accepted index; a copied manifest supplies no
authority. Program acceptance is a separate reviewable action under that Program's authority.
This contract reports no projection, adoption, declaration, orientation or currency observation.

What this baseline is, and is not. The collective is the conduit through which the owner's
direction reaches programs. What arrives through it may include expectations, directives,
and work assignments, and a program treats them as direction. The collective sets none of
them. It carries, distributes, and observes direction that originates with the owner, and
it never originates direction of its own.

The one expectation this release carries is participation: the owner expects each program
to participate in the collective and to adopt its latest release. A program chooses when
it adopts; it does not choose whether.

The collective grants no authority of its own and carries no credentials. It never merges
its own proposal into a program. This release says nothing about how work is reported or
budgeted.
