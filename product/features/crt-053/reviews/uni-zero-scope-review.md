**Gate**: scope-review | **Stance**: Right problem, right time — proceed; but resolve Q1 as a deliberate contract change and split #405 out (Q4) before this becomes a delivery brief.

### Vision / roadmap fit

This is squarely a `goal:self-learning` feature, and it touches the most load-bearing claim in the entire product vision: *"knowledge becomes trustworthy."* The Story section states it directly — "Agents stopped relitigating decisions." The observed failure in #704 (deprecated #4819/#4746 cited as authoritative during crt-052 scope creation, with intact active successors) is a live counterexample to that claim, surfaced by our own dogfooding. Fixing it defends the vision core, not a peripheral quality metric.

Sequencing against in-flight work is favorable. The self-learning queue right now is dominated by the attribution chain (vnc-030 #699 closed, #703/#707/#700 open) and graph-corpus health (#599, #538). crt-053 sits in a different code neighborhood (search.rs read path + nli_detection_tick write path) and does not contend with those. The one real adjacency the scope correctly flags: #606 (context_correct auto-redirects incoming graph *edges* to terminal active) already established "redirect to terminal active" as a precedent on the *write* side. crt-053's recommended Q1 answer (redirect-to-active on the *read* side) is the symmetric completion of that decision — a strong consistency argument the human should weigh in favor of redirect.

The Non-Goals are well-drawn and protect the roadmap: no fusion-weight re-tuning (ASS-037 #3984 stays authoritative), no NLI revival, no quarantine redesign, no #471 compaction pull-in. This keeps the diff to gating/filtering policy and prevents scope bleed into the intelligence-pipeline formula, which is exactly the boundary that should hold.

### Approach commentary

Strengths:

It explicitly retires #118's "supersession is ignored" claim (Step 6a/6b already exist) and re-frames #585 from "NLI" to "cosine-only" (since `w_nli=0.00`/`nli_enabled=false`). This is the difference between a feature and four bolted-together bug tickets, and it is done rigorously with file:line evidence.
- It correctly refuses to treat the four issues as one homogeneous bug. Splitting them across read-path / write-path / test-correctness is the right decomposition and de-risks delivery.
- It carries forward lesson #4536 as a hard acceptance criterion (AC-07). I verified #4536 — it is precisely on point (active-only status guards are invisible without a deprecated-superseded-by-cited-active regression test). Good knowledge reuse.
- C-04 (assert ranking/presence, never penalty constants — crt-013 ADR-003/#703) is the correct test discipline and is encoded as a constraint, not left to chance.

Concerns / things to weigh:
- **#704 is labeled `bug` but this is a contract change, and the scope says so honestly (Problem Framing #1).** "Penalize-but-keep-visible" was the deliberate crt-010 design intent. Changing it to exclude/redirect is a behavior change to a public MCP tool, not a regression fix. The human should ratify it *as* a contract change — including the small risk that some existing agent workflow relies on seeing penalized-but-present deprecated entries. AC-04 (context_get unchanged) is the right escape hatch, but the read-path contract shift deserves an explicit "yes, change the contract" from the gate, not an implicit one.
- **#405 dilutes the feature.** The scope itself concedes it is "only tangentially a status-signal problem" and is a confidence-pipeline timing flake. Bundling a test-isolation flake into a retrieval-contract feature invites a muddy diff and a muddy retro. I'd lift it out (see Q4 below).
- **#406 is scoped as "reproduce before fixing," which is correct, but it is also the one item with unknown root cause.** If root-causing reveals the failure is in Python-suite graph-snapshot *construction* (fixture/harness) rather than in retrieval, it may not belong in a self-learning feature either. Recommend treating #406's fix size as a discovery checkpoint, not a committed estimate, before this converts to a delivery brief.
- Cross-transport coverage is implicit but not asserted. The Non-Goals correctly exclude Strict/UDS-injection mode ("already drops non-Active"). Worth one explicit AC or note that the new Flexible-mode contract does not *diverge* the two modes' active-head resolution in a way that surprises (both should resolve to the same terminal active head; only default-visibility differs). Minor, but cheap to state.

### Recommended answers to open questions

- **Q1 (blocking — exclude / redirect / demote-with-note / keep):** Recommend **(b) redirect-to-active**, as the scope proposes. Rationale: it is the symmetric read-path completion of #606's already-shipped write-path "redirect incoming edges to terminal active" decision — the same mental model on both sides of the graph. It is the "least surprising" behavior per #704 (the agent gets the live decision it was actually looking for). (a) exclude-silently loses the signal that a related entry existed; (c) demote-with-note reintroduces the visible-dead-entry citation-hygiene gap this feature exists to close. Redirect is the only option that both removes the dead ID from default results *and* still answers the query.
- **Q2 (unconditional vs. config toggle):** Recommend **unconditional, no toggle**, per precedent #3630 (col-030 Contradicts suppression shipped with no toggle). A toggle on a trustworthiness guarantee is a footgun — it lets a deployment turn off the thing that makes citations trustworthy, and it doubles the test matrix forever. Consistency with #3630 wins.
- **Q3 (dangling chain — no reachable active terminal):** Recommend **exclude from default search** and require a regression test for the dangling case. Rationale: if redirect-to-active is the contract, a deprecated entry with no active terminal cannot honor it — and surfacing it penalized reopens exactly the citation-hygiene hole (an agent can still cite a dead, un-redirectable ID). context_get keeps it reachable for provenance (AC-04). Note this is the inverse of the bugfix-473 dangling case and should reuse whatever dangling-chain detection that work established, not invent a parallel one.
- **Q4 (#405 in scope as a fix, or split out?):** Recommend **split out** into its own bugfix issue (confidence-pipeline test-isolation), labeled `bug` not `goal:self-learning`. The scope's own language ("only tangentially a status-signal problem," "timing flake") makes the case. Keeping it here costs feature focus and muddies AC-06's signal. Splitting it is cheap and keeps crt-053 about the retrieval contract. If the human prefers to keep the issue count low, the fallback is to keep it but explicitly tag AC-06 as "incidental hardening, not the feature thesis."
- **Q5 (fresh ASS-037-style eval-harness check?):** Recommend **ranking-test + integration-suite coverage is sufficient — no new eval harness** — *with one condition*: because redirect changes recall (it removes entries from default results and injects heads), include at least one assertion that an *active*-entry query's top-5 is unchanged by the new gating (a cheap P@5-on-active sanity check, not a full ablation). The scope's own Q5 caveat ("unless redirect changes recall") triggers here — redirect does change recall by construction — so the lightweight guard is warranted, but a full 2356-scenario ablation is not.

### Recommended actions

1. **Ratify Q1 = redirect-to-active explicitly as a contract change** (not a bug fix) at the gate, and record the #606 symmetry as the rationale. This is the one decision everything else hangs on.
2. **Confirm Q2 = no toggle, Q3 = exclude dangling (with regression test).**
3. **Split #405 out of crt-053 (Q4)** as a standalone confidence/test-isolation bugfix; drop AC-06 or relabel it incidental.
4. **Treat #406 as a discovery checkpoint**: reproduce and root-cause first; if the cause is Python-suite harness/fixture rather than retrieval, re-evaluate whether it belongs in this feature or in test-infra.
5. **Add a lightweight active-query P@5 guard** to the test plan (Q5 condition) before this converts to an IMPLEMENTATION-BRIEF.
6. **Add one explicit assertion/note** that Flexible and Strict modes resolve to the *same* terminal active head (only default-visibility differs), to forestall a cross-mode divergence surprise.
7. Once Q1–Q3 are ratified, AC-01/AC-02/AC-03 become concrete and this is ready to proceed to design. The scope is otherwise delivery-ready in its framing quality.
