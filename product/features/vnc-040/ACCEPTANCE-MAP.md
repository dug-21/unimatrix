## Coverage Notes

- All SCOPE ACs (AC-01…AC-09) are present; AC-08 is split into AC-08a (per-file, FR-10) and AC-08b (post-merge, FR-07/
  SR-01) per the spec, reflecting the #3905 third-layer re-validation carry-item. AC-10 (per-slug `instructions`, FR-14)
  is added per the design-gate correction (#785's explicit per-slug knob). AC-11 (canonical-classification drift guard,
  FR-16) is added per the ADR-004 (#5210) canonical per-slug-vs-global classification change (Option 2).
- AC-02 is strengthened to assert `Arc::ptr_eq` on the 3 global handles on the no-file fallthrough arm (machine-checked
  same-allocation, matching crt-056 AC-2), not mere value/byte-equality.
- The full-call-site closed verdict (AC-07) is itself a verifiable requirement covering the ENTIRE `build_project_server`
  surface — the 9 crt-056 params + `embed_handle` + `permissive` (GLOBAL-LOCKED) + `instructions` (OVERLAYABLE) + the
  `[embedding]` section — derived from the live signature, none omitted (R-07 / crt-056 AC-1 mirror). The framing is "no
  call-site input is absent," NOT a count.
- AC-11 is the machine-checked proof obligation for R-14 (classification↔`merge_configs`↔seed drift, High, crt-031
  pattern). It does NOT rewrite `merge_configs` — the registry is data-only and the drift-guard test is the binding.
- Risk traceability: 14 risks → 32 scenarios (RISK-TEST-STRATEGY.md). R-14 (High proof obligation) is added; its proof is
  AC-11. R-13 (stray global-locked section silently ignored — the NO-RUNTIME-WARN behavior) remains an ACCEPTED RESIDUAL,
  documented and not test-gated; but the per-slug-vs-global SPLIT is now OWNED by A's canonical classification (no longer
  unowned/hand-duplicated). Its mitigation is the one-way Feature B hand-off — B RENDERS seed annotations FROM A's
  `PER_SLUG_CONFIG_CLASSIFICATION`, never a third copy (cross-feature contract, see IMPLEMENTATION-BRIEF.md). SR-06 is a
  design-recorded decision (no behavioral test). FR-13 (`adapt_service` left default) is a recorded design decision, not
  an AC.
